## Full brief (E-E-A-T)

### Experience — how the film is built

The narrator’s take stays untouched: no cuts, no added pauses. The picture adapts to the voice, so every beat lands on a spoken word. Everything lives on one continuous sheet that the camera glides across under the voice.

### Expertise — craft

- Structure is drawn with an outline→fill trace wave.
- Heroes arrive as paper cut-outs with real shadows.
- Notes are hand-written by marker.

### Trust — what is forbidden

- Nothing appears with a fade or a wipe.
- Graphics never repeat what the voice says.
- Sheet text stays sharp at rest.
- The end card never fades.
